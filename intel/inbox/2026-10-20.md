## Compaction resends the skill list, skill hooks are capped

- Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/claude-code/main/CHANGELOG.md
- Published: 2.1.304
- What changed: After a compaction Claude is sent the skill list again, and `/reload-skills` now sends only new or changed skills. The skills of one source can register at most 1,024 hooks per session.
- Touches: R-SESS-007, plugins/sdlc-core/hooks/hooks.json
- Action: none
- Why: The factory registers a handful of hooks and keeps state in files, so neither change affects a rule or a plugin.

## Subagent and hook fixes in 2.1.304

- Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/claude-code/main/CHANGELOG.md
